# Day 1 — preliminary brand and domain screening

**Date:** July 27, 2026  
**Scope:** Rapid desk research, not legal trademark advice or a guarantee of
domain availability.

## Decision

**Do not use “ProofLayer” as a customer-facing brand.** It may remain an
internal code name. Do not buy a domain, invest in a logo, or file a trademark
under this name.

## Rationale

The July 27 search found active uses at [proof-layer.com](https://www.proof-layer.com/),
[prooflayer.tech](https://prooflayer.tech/), and
[prooflayer.app](https://www.prooflayer.app/), including a company operating in
AI red teaming and MCP security. This creates category, search, trademark
confusion, and sales risk.

## Required checks before purchase or filing

1. Check current `.com`, `.io`, `.dev`, and relevant country-domain
   availability through ICANN/RDAP and a reputable registrar.
2. Search exact and similar names in TURKPATENT, EUIPO, and WIPO databases.
3. Confirm software and cybersecurity coverage, especially Nice classes 9 and
   42, with qualified counsel.
4. Check LinkedIn, GitHub, npm, PyPI, and key social accounts.
5. Test pronunciation, spelling, mishearing, and cross-language associations
   with five users.
6. Reserve the domain and accounts only after these checks pass.
