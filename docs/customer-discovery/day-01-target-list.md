# Day 1 — target interviewee list

The initial [target-interviewees.csv](target-interviewees.csv) contains 30
candidate accounts and roles. It deliberately avoids inventing personal names;
the current person and role must be verified through the company site or an
authorized professional channel before outreach.

## Priority order

1. **A — 5 candidates:** five MSSPs.
2. **B — 10 candidates:** five channel or telecom companies and five fintechs.
3. **C — 15 candidates:** SaaS, technology, e-commerce, and mid-sized banks.

If the founder has up to five warm SOC contacts, add them to the same CSV with
priority `A` and place them ahead of cold outreach.

## Initial interview target

By Day 14: 10 completed interviews, at least five strong problem signals, at
least two demo or pilot candidates, and three concrete stories of telemetry,
parser, or detection failures.

## Target roles

- SOC Manager
- Detection Engineer or Detection Engineering Lead
- SIEM Engineer
- Security Operations Manager
- MSSP Service Delivery or SOC Director
- Security Architect

## Pre-outreach verification

- Is the person still at the company?
- Is the role appropriate for discovery?
- Is the SOC or SIEM footprint supported by public information?
- Is a warm introduction available?
- Are we using an authorized professional channel rather than personal data?

## Status flow

`candidate → identity verified → message ready → sent → response → interview
scheduled → interview complete → demo/pilot candidate`
